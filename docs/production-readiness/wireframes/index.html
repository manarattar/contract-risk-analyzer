Tell us what you need to review

Choose a file first. In the proposed product, nothing is sent until you start processing.

Proposed pilot: PDF with selectable text, DOCX or UTF-8 TXT. Maximum 10 MiB and 50 pages. Scanned or password-protected files may need a replacement.

This local prototype does not read, transmit or save your selection.

Privacy policy — proposal awaiting verification

Access would be limited to authorized workspace members. Relevant text may be sent to an approved AI provider. The provider, processing region and retention terms must be named here before confidential upload is enabled.

Proposed active retention: 30 days. Requested deletion: access revoked immediately, active copies purged within 24 hours; backup expiry within 30 days. These are design targets, not current guarantees.

Alternative states: invalid file, duplicate and missing context

Invalid file: “This file does not match its extension. Choose a valid PDF, DOCX or TXT.” Link the error summary to the file field.

Duplicate: “These bytes match a review in your workspace. Open that review or create a new version/context.” Never reveal another workspace's documents.

Unknown context: permit extraction and neutral questions; withhold context-dependent conclusions. Preserve the form if the upload fails.

Interaction and focus notes

Native file picker is the keyboard alternative to optional drag/drop. Start is an explicit action after privacy and context. Validation focuses an error summary, then linked fields; do not move focus on selection. Show byte-transfer progress separately from analysis. Mobile stacks file/privacy before context. Production must replace policy placeholders with approved facts.

Loading, error and focus notes

Production status uses a polite live region and actual stage counters. No fabricated progress or fixed promises. User-triggered failure focuses its heading; automatic progress never steals focus. Cancel waits for worker acknowledgement and is distinct from deletion. Retry keeps the same job context and reports whether provider cost may recur. Polling cleans up and backs off when hidden or disconnected.

Why this may matter

A cap based on thirty days of fees may be smaller than the loss you want covered. Check amounts, exceptions, related provisions and your business requirements before deciding.

Suggested action — for human review

Confirm the intended cap with your legal reviewer. Any replacement wording must preserve the intended exceptions and be checked against the rest of the agreement.

Production: keep AI original, reviewer diff, author and timestamp. A failed save preserves your draft; conflicting edits require reconciliation.

Ask about this document

Example supported answer: Section 8.1 bases the cap on fees paid during the preceding thirty days. Source: page 6, section 8.1.

If asked about enforceability or an unsupported fact: explain what cannot be established. Retrieval failure shows retry, never a canned answer.

Alignment and review controls

Illustrative direct match

Repeated and split clauses need many-to-many matching. A missing counterpart is “unmatched” until coverage is confirmed.

Loading, empty and error variants

Loading: show each version's parse status; preserve selections. No changes: report no changes found in matched, readable scope, not equal legal risk. Partial: identify unreadable pages and uncertain matches. Error: retry alignment on saved versions. Distinguish cosmetic changes, moved sections, added/removed clauses and changed obligations.

Mobile and keyboard notes

Below 600 px versions stack with persistent baseline/revised labels and paired-location links. Deletion/addition wording is also named in text, not color alone. Native disclosure controls provide keyboard access to uncertain alignment. Production preserves the selected change when moving between source versions and never forces synchronized scroll. Both versions require authorization.

Deleting should immediately revoke app access, then purge active copies. Backups and provider copies may follow separate approved schedules. Downloaded copies outside this app cannot be recalled.

Export and deletion states

Export loading announces progress; error preserves scope and offers retry; empty selection explains why generation is unavailable. A generated private link expires and checks ownership on access. Delete confirmation names scope, affected versions and dependent comparisons. Revocation and purge are separate statuses. On successful navigation away, focus returns to the library status heading. Mobile stacks export before deletion; destructive action remains explicitly separated.
